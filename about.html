Institutional profile

A private university in Abuja

Canadian University of Nigeria is a private university in Abuja, Federal Capital Territory. The National Universities Commission lists the institution among Nigeria's private universities and records its establishment in 2023.

The university's public-facing identity is built around the tagline “Where Knowledge Meets Global Excellence” and an academic offering spanning health sciences, computing, and management & social sciences.

History

The institution was established in 2023 and received a provisional operating licence recorded by NUC.
