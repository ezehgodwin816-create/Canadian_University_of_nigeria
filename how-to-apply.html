How to Apply

Follow the complete digital application journey.

Payment note. Never pay a third party who asks for your password or requests payment outside an official university-approved channel. The student payment gateway on this platform uses Paystack only after a valid fee record is available.

Troubleshooting

Application will not submit

Check required fields, sign in again if your session expired, and retry. If the problem continues, use the Contact page with the application number shown in your portal.

Document upload failed

Use a supported file format and a clear file name. The secure application document area stores files under your authenticated account.

Payment issue

Check the fee record in your portal. Do not submit a second payment until the first transaction's status is clear.

Forgot your application number?

Sign in to the applicant/student portal to review your application history, or contact Admissions with the email used during application.
