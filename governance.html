Governance

Governance framework

Core governance bodies and the responsibilities they normally carry within a Nigerian university framework.

Governing Council

Provides institutional oversight, strategic direction and stewardship in accordance with the university's governing instruments.

Senate

Provides academic governance over programmes, examinations, standards and academic regulations.

University Management

Coordinates day-to-day institutional administration, implementation of approved policy and operational delivery.

Academic & administrative committees

Specialised committees support quality assurance, finance, admissions, research, student affairs and other institutional functions.

Published membership

The current public CUN website does not publish a complete current membership list for Council or Senate. This directory deliberately avoids inventing names.
