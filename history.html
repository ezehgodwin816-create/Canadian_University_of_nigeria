University history

History

Key milestones in the establishment of Canadian University of Nigeria.

2023 — Establishment

The National Universities Commission lists Canadian University of Nigeria, Abuja among private universities established in 2023.

2023 — Provisional licence

NUC announced the issuance of provisional operating licences to new private universities, including Canadian University of Nigeria, Abuja.

2023 — Academic launch planning

Published launch coverage described the university's initial academic areas and programme portfolio.

2024 — Academic activity period

Public launch reporting stated that the university planned to commence academic activities in September 2024.

Sources. Regulatory history is based on National Universities Commission records; academic-launch details are based on published launch reporting.
