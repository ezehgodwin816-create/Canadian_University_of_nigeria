Scholarships

Scholarship guidance for prospective and current students.

Verified-opportunity policy. Only scholarships published by CUN or a clearly identified legitimate external provider should be treated as current. This site does not invent scholarship amounts or deadlines.

University scholarships

When CUN publishes a scholarship or fee-support scheme, the opportunity should specify eligibility, deadline, documents and application instructions.

External opportunities

External scholarships can be considered where the provider is legitimate and the application route can be verified independently.

Application checklist

  • Confirm eligibility before applying.
  • Use the provider's official application page.
  • Prepare academic records and identity documents.
  • Never pay a third party to guarantee a scholarship.
