Undergraduate admissions

Undergraduate Admissions

Choose the right entry route, prepare your records and complete the online application.

UTME

Public launch reporting identifies UTME admission for undergraduate candidates. Use the current CUN admission notice for the exact JAMB subject combination and cut-off applicable to your programme.

Direct Entry

Applicants with eligible prior qualifications should submit the credentials required under the current CUN Direct Entry policy.

Transfer

Transfer applicants should provide previous university records and follow the current credit-evaluation procedure issued by Admissions.

Required records

Academic

Relevant secondary-school results and, where applicable, prior tertiary qualifications.

Identity

Valid personal identification and the personal details requested by the application form.

Programme-specific requirements. CUN's public website does not currently publish a single universal cut-off table for every programme. Confirm the current requirement for your chosen programme before submission.
