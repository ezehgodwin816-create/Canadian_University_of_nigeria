About the University

A vision for excellence in Abuja

This digital platform presents a modern identity for Canadian University of Nigeria — combining global academic standards with a strong Nigerian and African focus.

Mission

To provide transformative education that develops ethical leaders, innovators and professionals equipped for a globalised world.

Vision

To be recognised as a leading centre of academic excellence, research and community impact in West Africa.

Values

Integrity, excellence, innovation, inclusivity and service. Student-centred learning and responsible global citizenship.
