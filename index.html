About the University

A official Vision for Excellence in Abuja

This digital platform presents a modern digital identity for Canadian University of Nigeria — combining global academic standards with a strong Nigerian and African focus.

Mission

To provide transformative education that develops ethical leaders, innovators and professionals equipped for a globalised world. (Institutional information)

Vision

To be recognised as a leading centre of academic excellence, research and community impact in West Africa. (Institutional information)

Values

Integrity, excellence, innovation, inclusivity and service. Student-centred learning and responsible global citizenship. (Institutional information)
