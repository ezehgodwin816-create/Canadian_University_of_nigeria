NUC lists Canadian University of Nigeria, Abuja among Nigeria's private universities. The NUC's 2023 announcement recorded the issuance of a provisional licence.

Programme-level

Programme status

Programme approval and professional accreditation are separate from the institution's operating status. Always verify the current status of the specific programme with the relevant regulator before making a decision.

Important. This website does not use unsupported claims such as “fully accredited” for every programme. Accreditation status is maintained at the level published by the relevant regulatory body.
