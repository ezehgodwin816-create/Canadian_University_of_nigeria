Governance & Trust

Cookies & Local Storage

Draft for counsel review. This notice should be updated to match the final analytics, consent and authentication stack.

Essential storage

The platform may use browser storage for temporary form state and interface preferences. Production authentication should use the configured authentication provider rather than storing passwords in browser storage.

Analytics & optional technologies

No analytics provider should be described as active until it is actually deployed. Any non-essential cookies or similar technologies should be disclosed and, where required, activated only after appropriate consent.

Third-party services

Payment, authentication, map, video, analytics and support providers should be documented with links to their current privacy/cookie information where required.
