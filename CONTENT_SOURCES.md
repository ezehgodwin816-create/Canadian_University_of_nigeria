# Content Sources

The public content in this build is based on the following sources checked during the September 2026 build pass:

1. Canadian University of Nigeria official public website: https://www.cun.edu.ng/
2. National Universities Commission — Private Universities directory: https://www.nuc.edu.ng/nigerian-univerisities/private-univeristies/
3. NUC public notice on provisional licences for new private universities (2023), including Canadian University of Nigeria: https://www.nuc.edu.ng/nuc-issues-provisional-license-to-37-new-private-varsities/
4. PRNigeria / published launch reporting describing CUN's initial academic programmes: https://prnigeria.com/2023/11/15/years-students-will-prefer/
5. The Guardian Nigeria reporting the same initial academic programme groupings: https://guardian.ng/education/nigerians-wont-travel-abroad-for-education-from-next-decade-prof-gwarzo/
6. NUCIS public course directory, used only where its current catalogue directly identifies CUN courses: https://nucis.ng/explore/courses/filter%26meta%3Dcourse_qualification_type%21is_custom_checkbox%3AUndergraduate

Unverified third-party phone numbers, fees, leadership lists, social accounts and accreditation claims were deliberately excluded.
